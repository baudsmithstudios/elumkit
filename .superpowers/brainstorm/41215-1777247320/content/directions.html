Pergyl — three design directions

Tagline candidate: "HTML-first web UI primitives with a clean terminal feel."

Constraints I'm reading from the project

  • Anti-retro: README rules out scanlines, pixel art, CRT glow — so the umberrelay phosphor treatment is off the table.
  • Monospace-forward: JetBrains Mono / IBM Plex Mono is the product's typeface. The banner should use it too, not borrow Cinzel from vigil.
  • Aponis rust accent: #8b4a2a (light) / #c47a5a (dark). This is the only chromatic note — everything else is neutrals.
  • Calm and dense: "Clean terminal feel" — instrument, not decoration. Vigil's corner-tick grammar is closer to right than umberrelay's panel chrome.

Three directions — click one to mark a preference

A. Sibling of Vigil — same chrome, mono type, rust ink

Inherit vigil's corner-tick + flanking-rule frame so the three projects feel related, but swap Cinzel for JetBrains Mono and teal for Pergyl's rust accent. Quietest of the three. Ascetic.

Pro: family resemblance to vigil; truly anti-retro. Con: reads as "vigil but rust" if we don't push further.

B. Eat your own dog food — banner is a Pergyl card

The README banner is built from actual Pergyl primitives — a labeled card with a status label, title, and subtitle. The product demonstrates itself in the first impression.

Pro: strongest identity — the banner is the product. Con: denser; needs care so the social card (1280×640) doesn't feel sparse.

C. ASCII chrome — box-drawing frame, no decoration

Frame is rendered with Unicode box-drawing characters (or stroked SVG approximations of them). Most overtly "terminal" of the three without resorting to glow or scanlines.

Pro: distinctive; couldn't be mistaken for any other project. Con: can feel novelty if pushed too hard; needs careful kerning at every size.

My recommendation: B (eat your own dog food) for the social card, with the title banner a tighter restatement of the same composition. It says more about what Pergyl is than A or C, and avoids reading as a vigil reskin. Happy to defend any of the three — what's your gut?
