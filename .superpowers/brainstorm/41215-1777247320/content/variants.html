V1. Single card, scaled up

Banner and social card are the same composition at different scales. Quiet, focused, instantly readable.

Pro: coherence across surfaces; minimal moving parts. Con: social card has a lot of empty space; doesn't show what Pergyl can do beyond the card primitive.

V2. Banner + demo strip on the social card

Title card stays the focal point; a row of three small cards underneath shows status labels, a meter, and controls — a one-glance "what's in the box."

Pro: the social card actually previews the library. Con: more surface area to maintain; risks looking like a screenshot rather than a logo.

My take: V1 for purity, V2 for marketing punch. Since the social card shows up on link previews where you have ~2 seconds of attention, V2 earns its complexity — it's the only chance to say "this is a component library" without words. But if you'd rather the social card stay quiet and let the README do the demo work, V1 is honest.
